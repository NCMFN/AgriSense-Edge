## Slide 2: The Problem (The Water & Food Dilemma)
* **The Reality:** 33+ million smallholder farmers produce >70% of Sub-Saharan Africa's food supply.
* **The Shock:** Climate change has increased drought frequency twofold; 40% of irrigation water is lost to manual overwatering or guesswork.
* **The Market Failure:** Industrial agritech solutions are designed for large Western farms:
  * Prohibitive hardware cost (\$1,000+)
  * Require uninterrupted 4G/5G broadband
  * Demand high monthly SaaS subscription fees
* **Result:** Rural smallholders remain locked out of climate adaptation tools.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (AgriSense-Edge)
* **What it is:** An ultra-low-power, solar-harvesting Edge-AI sensor node & gateway network.
* **How it works:**
  1. Low-cost capacitive probes measure real-time root hydration and temperature.
  2. An on-device TinyML model forecasts crop water stress without internet access.
  3. Nodes communicate across a 5km radius over a license-free LoRa mesh to an automated solar valve.
  4. Farmers receive SMS/USSD alerts in their local dialect on basic feature phones.

---

## Slide 4: Breakthrough Technology (Edge-AI + LoRa)
* **Zero-Cloud Computing:** Intelligence resides in an optimized, quantized TinyML model running on a \$4 ESP32 microcontroller.
* **Energy Positive:** 22 µA deep-sleep current draw; 5W solar micro-panel provides indefinite off-grid autonomy.
* **Radical Inclusivity:** Zero app downloads; zero smartphone requirement.

---

## Slide 5: Validation & KPIs (Bench & Lab Results)
* **Water Reduction:** 38.2% reduction in water consumption verified under simulated dry-down soil cycles.
* **Hardware BOM Cost:** Single prototype built for \$65; target batch manufacturing cost under \$45.
* **Inference Latency:** 14.8 ms on-device execution time.
* **Communication:** Reliable LoRa telemetry achieved across 2.5 km through non-line-of-sight rural foliage.
